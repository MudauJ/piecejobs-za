## 5. WORKER AND HOMEOWNER AGREEMENTS
Create two new pages:

### Worker Agreement (/#/worker-agreement)
A professional legal agreement between PieceJobs ZA and workers covering:
- Independent contractor status (NOT an employee of PieceJobs ZA)
- Payment terms (released within 24 hours of job completion)
- Platform fee deduction (15%)
- ID verification requirement
- Code of conduct (punctuality, professionalism, no harassment)
- Dispute resolution process
- Account termination conditions
- Governing law: South Africa
- POPIA compliance
- Digital acceptance (checkbox "I agree to the Worker Agreement" on registration)

### Homeowner Agreement (/#/homeowner-agreement)  
A professional legal agreement between PieceJobs ZA and homeowners covering:
- Payment held in escrow until job completion
- Refund policy (full refund if worker doesn't show, partial if job partially done)
- Homeowner responsibilities (safe working environment, accurate job description)
- Rating and review obligations
- Dispute resolution process
- Prohibited conduct (requesting work outside platform to avoid fees)
- Account termination conditions
- Governing law: South Africa
- Digital acceptance (checkbox "I agree to the Homeowner Agreement" on registration)

Add links to both agreements:
- In footer: "Worker Agreement" | "Homeowner Agreement"
